01简介Profile

让机械系统更可靠，
也更有感知力。

Making mechanical systems
more reliable—and perceptive.

宋佳楠，工学博士，中南大学机电工程学院副教授、博士生导师。毕业于北京航空航天大学，并曾获国家留学基金委资助赴英国拉夫堡大学联合培养。

主持及参与国家自然科学基金、湖南省自然科学基金及多项航空航天相关研究项目。已发表 SCI 论文 30 余篇，并长期担任相关国际期刊审稿人。

Jianan Song is an Associate Professor and PhD supervisor at Central South University. He received his PhD from Beihang University and was a CSC-sponsored visiting doctoral researcher at Loughborough University.

He leads and contributes to research funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Hunan Provincial Natural Science Foundation, and aerospace-industry programmes. He has published more than 30 SCI-indexed papers.

01

一体化高温薄膜传感器

Integrated high-temperature thin-film sensors

采用直写、喷墨打印等技术，发展面向航空航天极端高温环境的温度、热流与应变传感器，并探索界面强度调控方法。

Direct-writing and inkjet-printing routes for temperature, heat-flux and strain sensors, with an emphasis on long-term interface integrity.

SENSING
02

热障、耐磨与隐身涂层

Thermal-barrier, wear-resistant and stealth coatings

研究航空航天高温部件涂层的剥落与失效机理，建立寿命预测、强度评价和结构设计方法。

Failure mechanisms, life prediction, strength assessment and structural design of functional coatings for high-temperature components.

COATINGS
03

复杂机械数字孪生与健康管理

Digital twins and health management

利用机器学习和故障案例知识库，开展复杂装备的故障诊断、健康管理与人机交互研究。

Machine-learning-enabled fault diagnosis, case-based reasoning, health management and human–machine interfaces for complex equipment.

AI × MECHANICS
